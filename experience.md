## Research

In summer 2026, I took part in a National Science Foundation REU at the University at Albany, working with [Dr. Lu Li](https://www.albany.edu/cnse/faculty/lu-li) and Jiawei Wang. I focused on how indoor air quality (IAQ) varies across different indoor environments, comparing fine particulate matter (PM<sub>2.5</sub>) in classrooms, conference rooms, and kitchens during an academic period and a break period. 

I created indoor airflow simulations using Rhino 8, a 3D building model and the Grasshopper plugin, and cleaned and analyzed the data using Python. We found that indoor PM<sub>2.5</sub> dropped during the break and that outdoor infiltration alone couldn't explain indoor levels, which points to indoor activity as a major factor. This work became a co-authored conference paper. 

I plan to attend the [ASHRAE 2027 Winter Conference](https://www.ashrae.org/conferences/2027-winter-conference) and continue this research beyond the REU and contribute to practical solutions for air quality management.
